The point of sale that listens.

ioPOS turns an iPhone into a register for coffee carts, food trucks, market stalls and small shops. Say the order out loud, photograph your paper menu to set up, and ask plain questions about your sales — all on the phone in your hand.

Talk to Order. “Two large oat lattes and a croissant” becomes a cart in a second, understood on the iPhone.
Magic Setup. Photograph your menu board and ioPOS builds your items, sizes and prices for you to check.
Ask ioPOS. “How did we do today?” “We're out of croissants.” Real answers from your own numbers, and one-tap actions.
Everything a counter needs. Tips, discounts, split bills, cash with change, refunds, daily close, customers, stock and a team with PINs. Works offline and syncs when you're back online.

Try ioPOS Pro free for one week, then continue monthly or yearly. Cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings.
