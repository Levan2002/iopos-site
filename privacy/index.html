Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

ioPOS is made by Levani Topchishvili (“we”). This policy explains what the ioPOS app for iPhone collects, why, where it is stored and the choices you have. In short: your business data belongs to you, we don't sell it, we don't show ads and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

1. What ioPOS stores

  • Your account. If you sign in, your email address (or the private relay address from Sign in with Apple) and a user ID, so we can sign you in and keep your business in sync across your devices.
  • Your business data. What you enter to run your business: business name, menu items, prices and photos, taxes, orders, payments (amount, method, tip, and for card payments the card brand and last four digits — never full card numbers), refunds, stock levels, your team members and their roles, and device names.
  • Your customers' details, if you choose to add them: name, phone, email, notes, purchase history and whether they agreed to receive offers. You are responsible for having your customers' permission to store their details, and ioPOS only uses them to run your business (for example, texting a receipt).
  • Subscription status. Whether your business has ioPOS Pro and your AI credit balance. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment card.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Talk to Order (speech to an order), Magic Setup (reading a menu photo), barcode scanning and Ask ioPOS run on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your voice, photos and questions are not sent to us. Speech recognition uses Apple's Speech framework under Apple's privacy policy. PINs for locking a shared iPhone are stored only on that iPhone, as a salted hash in the Keychain, and are never synced.

3. Where data goes and who processes it

  • Supabase (database and sign-in, hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States) stores your account and business data when you sign in.
  • PowerSync (JourneyApps) syncs that data between your devices and the database, in the United States.
  • Apple processes subscriptions and in-app purchases, and Sign in with Apple.
  • RevenueCat records your subscription and purchases under an anonymous ID for your business, so ioPOS Pro works on all of your business's devices.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. We don't sell or rent personal information, don't use it for advertising, and don't share it with data brokers.

4. Why we use it

To provide ioPOS: running your register, syncing your devices, restoring your data on a new iPhone, letting your team work together, providing ioPOS Pro and AI credits, and keeping the service secure. If ioPOS later adds AI features that run in the cloud, the app will explain what is sent and ask for your consent before using them.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you use ioPOS without signing in, it stays only on your iPhone.

6. Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in and every business you are the only owner of, with all of its data, from our servers, and removes it from your iPhone. You can also remove data from one iPhone without deleting your account. Deletion from backups completes within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data in the app, or ask us through the support page. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have further rights, including to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our providers, and access is limited to members of your business by database row-level security.

9. Children

ioPOS is a business tool and is not directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support page.
