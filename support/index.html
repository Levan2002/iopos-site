Common questions

How does the free trial work?
Tap Charge on your first sale and start the 1-week free trial of ioPOS Pro. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
Can I use ioPOS offline?
Yes. Sales are saved on your iPhone and sync when you're back online.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Sign in under Settings → Account & sync on the old iPhone, then choose “Already use ioPOS? Sign in” on the new one.
How do I add my team?
Settings → Team → Invite. Share the code or QR; your teammate enters it after signing in.
How do I restore my purchase?
Settings → ioPOS Pro → Restore purchases.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This deletes your data from our servers. See the Privacy Policy.
